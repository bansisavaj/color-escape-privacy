Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you play our puzzle game.

Effective date: October 23, 2026
Developer: Bansi Savaj

Bansi Savaj ("we," "our," or "us") provides Color Escape 3D (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App, including information processed by our advertising partner, Google AdMob.

1. Game accounts and progress

You can play Color Escape 3D without creating an account or signing in. Your game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a game-progress cloud account or receive your locally stored progress through an account service.

Removing the App may remove locally stored game progress. Device backup or restore behavior may depend on your Android settings and device services.

2. Advertising and information collected

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. When ads are loaded or shown, Google's advertising technology may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • Device and advertising identifiers, where available and permitted;
  • App interactions and advertising activity, such as ad views, taps, and performance;
  • Diagnostic information, including crash and performance data.

Google may use this information to deliver and measure advertisements, support ad personalization when permitted, detect fraud and abuse, and maintain its services. The information Google processes and the purposes for processing can depend on your device, region, consent choices, and Google's policies.

For more information, read Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

3. Advertising consent and choices

The App includes an advertising consent process where required. Depending on your region and applicable law, you may be asked to choose whether certain data may be used for personalized advertising. Where the App makes privacy options available, you can review or change your choices through that process.

You may also be able to manage advertising preferences or reset or delete your advertising ID through your Android device settings. Available controls vary by Android version and region. Non-personalized advertising may still involve limited data processing for ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

4. Sharing with third parties

We use Google AdMob as our advertising service provider. Data described above may be transmitted to and processed by Google and, depending on ad serving, its advertising partners in accordance with applicable consent choices and policies. We do not sell locally stored game progress or maintain a database of player accounts.

5. Children's privacy

Color Escape 3D is intended for a general audience, including families and children. Advertising shown to children is subject to applicable child-protection requirements. Where required, we configure advertising and consent handling to restrict personalized advertising and use age-appropriate ad treatment. Parents and guardians may contact us with privacy questions or concerns at the email below.

We do not ask players to provide their name, email address, or other contact details to play the game.

6. Data security

Game progress is kept on your device. Information transmitted through the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit, as described by Google. No internet transmission or storage method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention and deletion

Your local game progress remains on your device until removed through app or device controls, subject to any device backup settings. Because we do not maintain player accounts or a cloud game-progress database, we cannot directly delete progress stored only on your device.

Google independently manages retention and deletion of information processed by AdMob under its policies and available privacy controls. To learn more about Google's data controls, visit Google Data & Privacy.

For questions or requests relating to information we control, contact us using the email below.

8. International data processing

Google may process advertising-related information in countries other than your country of residence, subject to its privacy policies and applicable legal safeguards.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App, advertising services, or applicable requirements. We will post the updated policy on this page and revise the effective date when changes are made.
